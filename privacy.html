Statuzo privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Statuzo is an Android app that helps you save and share WhatsApp statuses and media. It is an independent app. It is not made by, or connected to, WhatsApp or Meta.

What Statuzo does with your files

Statuzo reads the WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) status and document folders that you choose, and photos, videos and pictures that you pick inside the app. All of this happens on your phone. Statuzo does not upload your files, statuses, contacts, phone numbers or messages anywhere.

When you tap Save, Statuzo copies the item into a "Statuzo" album on your phone. Sticker packs you make are stored inside the app and are shared with WhatsApp only when you tap Add to WhatsApp.

What Statuzo collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Statuzo does not sell or share any data.

Internet use

Statuzo uses the internet only to check for new versions of itself on GitHub (github.com). That request is a normal web request, so GitHub can see your IP address as it can for any website you visit. Statuzo adds no personal information to it. You can turn automatic checks off in Tools > Updates. Versions of Statuzo distributed through Google Play do not check for updates at all.

Permissions

  • Photos, videos and audio: to read items you pick and to save items to your phone.
  • Install packages: only in the direct-download version, to install a Statuzo update you approve.
  • Internet: only for the update check described above.

Children

Statuzo is a general tool and is not directed at children.
